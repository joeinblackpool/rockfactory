About Poulton and its storage needs

Poulton-le-Fylde sits inland, east of Blackpool, in Wyre borough. Its old market place and church give the centre a traditional feel, while much of the town is family housing, from older semis to more recent estates. The A586 links Poulton with central Blackpool.

The questions we hear from Poulton tend to be practical: where can the business stock go, how do we cope during a move, and what do we do with things that do not fit in a newer house?

Newer homes, less storage

Many newer homes are designed around open living spaces. They look great, but there is often less built-in storage than in older houses, garages can be narrow, and loft space may be limited or not boarded.

If that sounds like your home, it helps to separate what you need nearby from what you only need sometimes. Keep the everyday things at home. Things that suit storage include:

  • Spare furniture from a previous, larger house.
  • Baby and toddler equipment you are keeping for later.
  • Garden furniture and outdoor toys over winter.
  • Archive boxes of household and business paperwork.

For small amounts, our first-floor storage pods and rooms open in spring 2027 at guide prices from £10 a week. For more, start with a 150 sq ft ground-floor unit.

When the home business needs its own space

Plenty of Poulton households run a small business from home: a craft business at the kitchen table, a repair service in the garage, an online shop in the box room. At some point it starts to crowd out family life.

Signs it may be time to move the work out:

  • Stock or tools have spread into rooms meant for other things.
  • You would rather customers or couriers did not come to your home address.
  • You find it hard to switch off when work is always in the next room.

Our units come with lighting, power and Wi-Fi connected, and tenants can receive mail and clients. You can also use the Rock Factory address on your Google Business Profile. Our guide on moving your business out of your home walks through the decision, and unit ideas for tech and repair suits anyone fixing devices or electronics.

Market stalls and craft fairs

A market town naturally has its share of stallholders, and many Poulton people trade at markets, craft fairs and pop-up events across the Fylde and beyond. The problem is the same for all of them: gazebos, trestle tables, weights, display stands and boxes of stock that fill the car and the hallway between events.

A unit can become the place you load from. Pack the stall kit in one corner, stock on shelves in another, and keep a checklist on the wall so nothing is forgotten on market morning. With 24-hour access, early starts are no problem.

A roller-shutter unit makes loading quicker than carrying everything through a door. Our guides on selling at Blackpool markets and units for market traders have more practical tips. If you sell food, remember that cooking and hot food are not allowed in our units.

Local storage or central Blackpool: how to decide

If you visit often, a short journey is worth a lot. If you visit rarely, the terms and the space itself may matter more. Ask any provider you are comparing:

  • Can I work in the unit, or only store?
  • Is power metered fairly? We charge at the supplier rate with no markup.
  • What is the minimum term? Ours is one month.
  • Are there extra fees or deposits for utilities? We have none.

For running a business from a unit, check whether business rates apply. Ask us about the specific unit, and see our guide to business rates on small units and the official GOV.UK guidance below.

Questions

Can I have clients visit me at the unit?

Yes. Tenants can receive clients and mail at the unit, and there is a shared tea and coffee room and toilets. All business use needs our prior approval.

Will my removals firm be able to deliver straight to the unit?

Yes, you can arrange for them to deliver to your unit. Roller-shutter units make large items easier, and all units are on the ground floor.

Can I have more than one unit if my business grows?

Some units have interconnecting doors, so you can rent two or three adjoining units, subject to availability.

Can I sell food from my market stock unit?

Dry, packaged goods can be stored, but cooking and hot food are not allowed in the units. Ask us on WhatsApp about anything else food-related before you sign up.
