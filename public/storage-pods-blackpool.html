Why small
is the smart buy

Nobody wants storage. They want their spare room back. Here is how to get it for less.

Stop paying to store air

Empty floor space costs the same as full floor space. A pod sized to what you own beats a unit sized to what you might own one day.

The hallway tax

Boxes in the hall, bikes in the kitchen, stock on the stairs: you are already paying for storage, in space and patience. A pod moves the cost somewhere you can see it.

Weekly and monthly, side by side

A weekly price can look smaller than it is. We show both, so you budget for the real number and there are no surprises.

Room to change your mind

Start small. If you need more space later, a room or a ground-floor unit is in the same building, subject to availability.

Keep things, not decisions

Storage is brilliant for things you will use again. Sort first, store second, and you will need a smaller pod than you think.

Honest guide prices

What people
store in a pod

A storage pod or room is a simple, secure space for things you want to keep but do not want at home or in your shop.

Household storage

Furniture, boxes and belongings while you move house, renovate or declutter.

Students and seasonal items

Belongings over the summer, or bikes, garden furniture, decorations and sports kit out of season.

Business archives

Boxed records and paperwork you need to keep but not in your office.

Stock for small businesses

Light stock for online sellers and market traders who do not need a ground-floor unit.

Hobby and collection storage

Craft supplies, collections, records and books kept safe and organised.

Tools and equipment

Hand tools, event kit and equipment that fits in a smaller space.

Common
questions

When do the storage pods open?

Spring 2027. Register your interest on WhatsApp and we will message you as soon as dates, sizes and prices are confirmed.

Where are the storage pods?

On the first floor of The Old Rock Factory, Keswick Road, off Park Road, Blackpool FY1 5PB.

What sizes and prices will there be?

Pods of 15, 25 and 35 sq ft from £10 a week, and rooms of 50, 75 and 100 sq ft from £22 a week. These are guide prices, set around the middle of Blackpool storage prices, and will be confirmed before we open.

How do I get to the first floor?

By stairs. The pods and rooms suit boxes and items you can carry. For heavy or bulky items, our ground-floor units with roller-shutter access may suit you better.

I need storage before spring 2027. What can I do?

Our ground-floor units are available from 1 November, from £65 a week for 150 sq ft, with roller-shutter or security-door access. Pre-book and your first week is free.

What can't I store?

Nothing that is flammable, such as fuel, gas cylinders or solvents, and no cars. Low-risk items such as furniture, boxes, paper, fabric and dry goods are fine.
