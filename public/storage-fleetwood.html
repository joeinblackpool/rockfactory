Fleetwood and what its residents need

Fleetwood is a town with a strong identity: a fishing and port heritage, the Marine Hall, its lighthouses, its market and its position on the Wyre estuary. The tram line ends here, and the A585 is the main road into town. It is part of Wyre borough.

The housing includes older terraces near the docks and town centre, and larger homes and newer estates further out. Space at home is as tight here as anywhere, and people store things for all the usual reasons: moving, renovating, downsizing, running a business.

When a unit in central Blackpool makes sense from Fleetwood

We will be straight about this. If you need to visit your storage several times a day, a site close to home is likely to suit you better. But plenty of storage is visited rarely, and for that the journey matters much less.

A unit with us is more likely to work if:

  • You are storing things long-term and will only visit now and then.
  • Your work or daily routine already brings you into Blackpool.
  • You need a workspace with power and Wi-Fi, not just a store.
  • You want a rolling monthly agreement instead of a long lease.

Whatever you decide, check your route from Fleetwood on Google Maps first. We do not quote distances or journey times.

Long-term storage done well

If your things will sit for months at a time, packing them properly is worth the extra effort at the start:

  1. Clean and dry everythingDirt and moisture cause more damage than time does.
  2. Use strong boxes of the same sizeThey stack safely and are easy to label.
  3. Keep air movingLeave a gap between boxes and the walls, and avoid sealing fabrics in plastic.
  4. Make an inventoryKeep a list at home, with photos, so you know what is where without a trip.
  5. Check in occasionallyA visit every so often lets you spot problems early.

Our guides to packing a storage unit and archiving documents go further.

Fishing, boating and outdoor kit

With the sea and the estuary on the doorstep, many Fleetwood households have rods, tackle boxes, waders, wetsuits, kayaks or camping gear. Much of it is seasonal and it takes up a lot of room.

Rinse salt water off kit and let it dry fully before storing it, or you may come back to mould and corrosion. Keep anything with fuel, such as outboard motors or fuel cans, out of the building: bulk fuel is not allowed unless agreed in writing. Rods, tackle, clothing and non-powered craft that fit through the door or shutter are fine.

Questions

Is it worth storing things in Blackpool if I live in Fleetwood?

It can be if you will visit rarely, if you are in Blackpool regularly anyway, or if you need a workspace rather than just storage. If you will visit very often, a site closer to home may suit you better.

Can I store a small boat or kayak?

Non-powered kit that fits through the door or roller shutter is fine. Outboard motors and fuel are not allowed in bulk unless agreed in writing. Ask us on WhatsApp if you are unsure.

Can someone else visit the unit for me?

Talk to us about access arrangements before you sign up. The tenant is responsible for the unit and must provide valid government-issued photo ID.

What do I need to bring when I sign up?

Valid government-issued photo ID and a signed Direct Debit mandate, plus one month's rent in advance and a security deposit equal to one month's rent.

Is electricity included in the price?

Electricity is connected in every unit, and usage is charged separately at the supplier rate with no markup. There are no utility deposits.

Do you have a site in Fleetwood?

No. Our only building is on Keswick Road, off Park Road, in central Blackpool FY1 5PB.
