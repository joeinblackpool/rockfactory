Layton homes and the space squeeze

Much of Layton is made up of terraced and semi-detached streets. They are practical family homes, but a garage, if there is one, is usually full within a year or two, and the loft is often hard to reach with anything heavier than a box of decorations.

When people from Layton ask us about space, it tends to be one of three things: a trade that has outgrown the van and the garage, a job done from the back bedroom that now needs a proper room, or a household with too much stuff and nowhere to put it. Each needs a slightly different unit, so we will take them in turn.

For tradespeople: get the materials out of the house

Plumbers, electricians, joiners, decorators and builders often keep stock and tools at home. That can mean ladders in the hall, offcuts in the garden and a van that has to be loaded from the pavement each morning.

A roller-shutter unit gives you one place for all of it. The 150 sq ft unit at £65 a week suits tools, fittings and smaller materials. The 180 sq ft unit at £78 a week (about £338 a month) gives more floor for timber, boxed kitchens or kit you rotate between jobs. With 24-hour access you can collect what you need before an early start.

A few practical limits apply. We do not allow bulk flammable liquids or gases unless agreed in writing, which includes fuel, solvent-based paints and gas cylinders. Wood, fittings, tiles, dry goods and hand tools are fine. There is standard single-phase power only, so check that any equipment you want to run will work on it.

Our page on unit ideas for trades goes into more detail.

For home workers who need a door to close

Working from a spare room is fine until it is not. Perhaps the family needs the room back, or you need to meet clients somewhere other than your kitchen.

The 160 sq ft unit has a security door rather than a roller shutter, which makes it feel more like a room than a garage. It is windowless, but it has lighting, power and Wi-Fi connected. Tenants can receive mail and clients at the unit, and there is a shared tea and coffee room and toilets. If you would prefer natural light, our two offices at £70 a week each will be available from January 2027 after refurbishment.

Our guide on working comfortably in a windowless unit is worth a read before you decide, and so is moving your business out of your home.

For families with a full garage

Bikes, garden furniture, the cot that might be needed again, boxes from the last move: it builds up. Before renting anything, it is worth a weekend of sorting, because storing things you do not want is money spent for nothing.

If you still need the space after that, a ground-floor unit or, from spring 2027, one of our first-floor storage pods or rooms may suit. Pods and rooms are reached by stairs and are at guide prices still to be confirmed, from £10 a week for a 15 sq ft pod. Our storage unit versus garage guide compares the two honestly.

Comparing a unit here with options nearer Layton

You may find industrial units or storage options closer to your street. Comparing like with like is the key. Ask each place:

  • Is power included and how is usage charged? Ours is charged at the supplier rate with no markup.
  • Is there Wi-Fi, and is it already connected? Ours is.
  • How long is the lease? Many commercial units ask for years. Ours is rolling monthly after a one-month minimum.
  • Can you use the address on your Google Business Profile? Our tenants can.
  • Can you grow into a bigger space without moving site? You can swap units here, subject to availability, and some units interconnect.

Business rates may apply to commercial units. Ask us about the specific unit, and check the official guidance on Small Business Rate Relief, linked below.

Questions

Can I store a van-load of building materials?

Yes, as long as they are not bulk flammables such as fuel, solvent-based paints or gas cylinders, unless agreed in writing. Timber, tiles, fittings and dry materials are fine.

Can I run power tools in the unit?

Every unit has standard single-phase electricity, not three-phase. Light workshop use may be possible with our prior approval, so ask us about what you plan to do.

Can I use the unit as my business address?

Yes. Tenants can receive mail at the unit and can use the Rock Factory address on their Google Business Profile.

Do you have a site in Layton?

No. Our only building is on Keswick Road, off Park Road, in central Blackpool FY1 5PB.
