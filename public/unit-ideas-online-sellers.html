Ideas and how to set them up

E-commerce stock storage

A 150 sq ft roller-shutter unit holds a small online shop's stock, a packing bench and a label printer, with a clear lane for couriers.

  • Power needLow
  • LayoutSimple
  • Set-up costLow
  • Best unit150 sq ft or 180 sq ft

For sellers on Amazon, eBay, Etsy or their own Shopify store who have outgrown the house. Keep stock on shelving, a packing bench by the door and a label printer on Wi-Fi.

Technical profile

Equipment that fits
  • Steel boltless shelving bays along both long walls
  • A packing bench about 1.8 m long with a roll holder for tape and paper
  • Thermal label printer and parcel scale on the Wi-Fi
  • Sack truck or folding platform trolley
  • Clear lidded crates and shelf labels
Power and utilities

Everything runs from standard 13 amp sockets on the single-phase supply: lights, a laptop, a label printer, a scale and a phone charger. You will rarely need more than one double socket at the bench.

Safety and site rules

Boxed, dry stock is exactly what our units are set up for. There is no melting, heating or flammable processing, so the main things to manage are tidy aisles and not stacking cardboard against heaters. Tell us what you sell when you enquire, because every use needs our approval.

Floor plan

Vertical racking
Run shelving along both long walls and use the full height that your unit allows; we can tell you the height of the unit you are viewing. Heavy and fast-selling lines go at waist height, light bulky stock and spare packaging go on the top shelves, and slow sellers go low.
Mobile benches
Put the packing bench on lockable castors. Roll it to the middle when a big batch arrives for checking and labelling, then push it back against the wall by the door for everyday packing.
Flow of goods
Deliveries come in through the roller shutter and go straight onto a goods-in shelf for checking. Stock then moves to its labelled bay, orders are picked to the packing bench, and sealed parcels wait on a dispatch shelf next to the shutter for collection.

Launch checklist

Start-up supplies
  • Three to five shelving bays
  • Packing bench and stool
  • Label printer and labels
  • Parcel scale
  • Boxes, mailing bags, paper fill and tape
  • Shelf labels and a simple stock list
  • Sack truck
Insurance to discuss

Ask a broker about stock cover for the goods in the unit, goods in transit if you deliver yourself, and public liability if customers or couriers come to the unit. Employers' liability is a legal requirement in most cases once you take on staff.

Vintage and reseller clothing

Clothing rails, a photo corner and a steaming and packing table fit comfortably in 150 sq ft, and clothes are low-risk stock that is easy to organise.

  • Power needLow
  • LayoutSimple
  • Set-up costLow
  • Best unit150 sq ft or 160 sq ft

Clothing rails, a small photography corner and a packing desk for Vinted, Depop and eBay resellers. Clothes are low-risk stock and easy to organise on rails.

Technical profile

Equipment that fits
  • Heavy-duty double clothing rails on castors
  • Garment steamer
  • Plain backdrop or wall with two LED softbox lights
  • Mannequin or hanging photo rail
  • Packing table with mailing bags and tissue
Power and utilities

A steamer, two LED lights and a laptop all run from standard sockets. Keep the steamer on its own socket rather than a long extension lead.

Safety and site rules

Clothing is dry, low-risk stock. Keep bags and tissue in closed boxes and away from the steamer and any heater, and empty the steamer at the end of the day.

Floor plan

Vertical racking
Use rails for hung stock and wall shelving above them for folded items, shoes and bags in clear boxes. Label each rail by size, brand or platform so pickers find items fast.
Mobile benches
Wheeled rails let you change the room in minutes: push them together to open a photo area, or line them up by the door on dispatch day.
Flow of goods
Bags of new stock arrive at the door and go to a sorting rail. Items are cleaned and steamed, photographed in the corner, listed, and then hung on the stock rails. Sold items are picked, packed on the table and left on the dispatch shelf by the door.

Launch checklist

Start-up supplies
  • Two or three rails
  • Steamer
  • Two LED lights and a backdrop
  • Hangers and size dividers
  • Mailing bags, tissue and labels
  • Clear boxes for accessories
Insurance to discuss

Stock cover for clothing in the unit and public liability if buyers collect in person are worth discussing with a broker. Some platforms cover items in transit, so check what your selling platform includes.

  • Power needLow
  • LayoutModerate
  • Set-up costLow
  • Best unit180 sq ft or 300 sq ft

Store flat-pack boxes and dry items such as socks, stationery, books or packaged snacks, then pack and dispatch each month's boxes in batches.

Technical profile

Equipment that fits
  • Long folding tables set up as an assembly line
  • Shelving for products by month or box type
  • Pallet of flat-packed boxes stored upright
  • Label printer and scale
  • Tape guns and box cutters
Power and utilities

A label printer, a scale and a laptop on standard sockets cover the whole operation.

Safety and site rules

Dry boxed items such as socks, books, stationery and packaged snacks are fine. Ask us before including anything flammable, such as aerosols. Packaged food needs extra thought: see our dry goods and botanicals guide.

Floor plan

Vertical racking

Mobile benches

Flow of goods
Products arrive at the shutter and are checked into their bays. On packing day, boxes are made up at the start of the line, filled station by station, sealed, labelled and stacked on the dispatch side nearest the door.

  • Clipboard checklist for each box

Planning a small unit floor

Go up the walls, keep benches mobile and give goods one clear route from the door to the work area and back.

  • Measure before you buy racking. Ask us for the height of the unit you are viewing, then choose shelving that uses it safely, fixed to the wall where the manufacturer says so.
  • Heavy low, light high. Keep heavy stock and machines at or below waist height and light, bulky items on the top shelves.
  • Lockable castors. Benches and rails on lockable wheels let one room switch between making, photographing and packing.
  • One-way flow. Goods in at the door, work in the middle, finished orders back by the door. The 150, 180 and 300 sq ft units have roller shutters; the 160 sq ft unit has a security door.
  • Sockets first. Put machines near sockets and avoid chaining extension leads.

Why our units suit online sellers & e-commerce

  • Online selling is mostly dry, clean work with cardboard, paper and boxed goods, which is exactly the low-risk kind of business our units are made for.
  • Roller-shutter units make it easy to take in deliveries of stock and load outgoing parcels. The 160 sq ft unit with a security door suits smaller, higher-value stock.
  • Wi-Fi is connected in every unit, so you can print labels, update listings and answer customers from the unit.
  • Lighting, standard single-phase electricity and Wi-Fi are already connected in every unit (there is no three-phase power), with 24-hour access, free CCTV app access covering the entrance and the area outside the units, and shared facilities.

Not allowed in our units

Nothing that melts or heats combustible materials such as waxes, plastics, resins or oils, no flammable liquids or gases in bulk, and no car storage. Low-risk materials such as wood, paper, card, fabric and dry goods are fine. Every business use needs our approval first.

Start-up checklist

  1. Decide which unit suits your stock, machines and how you will load and unload.
  2. Message us with what your business does, so we can approve the use before you commit.
  3. Register your business with HMRC as a sole trader or limited company if you have not already.
  4. Speak to an insurance broker about public liability, contents and stock cover, and employers' liability if you have staff.
  5. Ask us whether business rates apply, and check Small Business Rate Relief on GOV.UK.
  6. Plan your layout: shelving up the walls, benches near sockets, and a clear route to the door.
  7. Arrange one month's rent, the deposit, photo ID and a Direct Debit mandate for move-in day.

Questions

Can I receive deliveries at the unit?

Yes. Tenants can receive mail and clients at the unit, and you have 24-hour access to meet couriers. Roller-shutter units make larger deliveries easier.

Which unit is best for an online shop?

For most sellers, 150 sq ft · roller shutter · £65 a week or 180 sq ft · roller shutter · £78 a week. If you also want a desk away from the stock, the 300 sq ft · two storeys with upstairs office · £130 a week has an upstairs office or storage area with a window.

Can I store stock that I sell on Amazon?

Yes, dry, boxed and low-risk stock is fine. Tell us what you sell when you enquire, because all business use needs our approval first.

What is included in the rent?

Lighting, electricity and Wi-Fi are already connected in every unit. Electricity usage is charged separately at the supplier rate, with no markup. Tenants get 24-hour access, free CCTV app access covering the entrance and the area outside the units, and shared facilities: a tea and coffee room, toilets and water.

Can I use the address on my Google Business Profile?

Yes. Tenants can use The Old Rock Factory, Keswick Road, Blackpool FY1 5PB as their business address on Google, so customers can find you without you putting your home address online.

Is there three-phase power?

No. Every unit has standard single-phase electricity, the same as a normal plug socket. Choose machines and tools that run on single-phase power.

What can't I do in a unit?

We do not allow anything that melts or heats combustible materials such as waxes, plastics, resins or oils, flammable liquids or gases in bulk, or car storage. Low-risk materials such as wood, paper, card, fabric and dry goods are fine. Every business use needs our approval.
