Useful information

Workspace
questions

Can I rent a unit as an artist’s studio?

Yes, artist studio use can be considered, subject to prior approval of your activity. The units have lighting, electricity and Wi-Fi already connected, but ground-floor spaces are windowless. Discuss your work and any specific requirements before choosing a unit.

Can I combine a workshop with storage?

The units can accommodate approved small business activity and general storage. Some have interconnecting doors, so you may be able to rent two or three adjoining units, subject to availability.

Can clients visit my business?

Yes. Tenants can receive clients and mail on site. Your proposed business activity needs prior approval.

Is electricity included in the weekly rent?

No. Lighting, electricity and Wi-Fi are already connected in the units, but electricity usage is charged separately at the standard supplier rate. We do not add a markup or make a profit on electricity.
