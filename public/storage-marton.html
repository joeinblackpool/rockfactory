Marton and the reasons people look for space

Marton covers a wide stretch of south-east Blackpool, with the green space of Stanley Park on one side and the Marton Mere nature reserve further out. The A583, Preston New Road, runs through the area and connects to junction 4 of the M55.

The housing is mostly suburban semis, bungalows and detached homes. People here are less likely to be desperate for basic space and more likely to need room for a project: a loft conversion, a new kitchen, a growing family, or a club whose kit has been living in someone's garage for years.

Extensions, kitchens and loft conversions

Home improvement projects are a common reason Marton households rent storage. When builders need a room empty, the contents have to go somewhere, and a garage full of furniture is soon a garage full of dust.

  • Plan the timing. Projects often overrun. Rolling monthly rent means you only pay for the months you use, with one month's notice when you leave.
  • Protect furniture properly. Use breathable covers rather than sealed plastic for wood and upholstery.
  • Keep a list. Note which boxes hold what, so you are not searching for the kettle on week three.
  • Think about access. 24-hour access means you can fetch things in the evening after work.

Our full guide to storage during a renovation covers this step by step.

Bikes, sports kit and family overflow

With parks and open spaces around, many Marton families have bikes, scooters, camping gear, paddleboards and garden furniture. Most of it is used in summer and is in the way for the rest of the year.

For a household that just needs a bit of overflow space, a ground-floor unit may be more than you need. Our storage pods and rooms, opening on the first floor in spring 2027, come in sizes from a 15 sq ft pod to a 100 sq ft room, at guide prices from £10 to £34 a week (to be confirmed). They are reached by stairs, so heavy items are better in a ground-floor unit. See also our guide to seasonal storage.

When the garage becomes a room

Converting an attached garage into a study, playroom or extra bedroom is a popular way to add space to a suburban home. The catch is that everything the garage used to hold still needs somewhere to live: the lawnmower, tools, paint tins, Christmas decorations and the boxes nobody has opened since the last move.

Before you rent anything, sort it. Some of it can go to the tip or a charity shop. Some belongs in a garden shed. What is left, the things you genuinely use but not every week, is what a storage unit is for.

A couple of cautions. Leftover tins of solvent-based paint, fuel for the mower and gas cylinders are not allowed in bulk at The Rock Factory unless agreed in writing, so dispose of them properly or keep them in a suitable outdoor store. Hand tools, garden furniture, boxed decorations and DIY materials such as timber are fine.

For clubs, teams and community groups

Sports teams, dance schools, scout and guide groups, amateur dramatics and other community groups all have the same problem: kit, costumes and equipment with no permanent home. Often it ends up spread across several members' houses.

A shared unit can bring it all together. A roller-shutter unit makes it easier to load nets, staging or display boards. You can also receive mail at the unit. Our page on units for clubs and community groups has ideas on sharing the cost and organising access. Ask us on WhatsApp how a group can rent a unit: move-in needs valid government-issued photo ID and a signed Direct Debit mandate.

Questions

How long can I keep things in storage during building work?

As long as you need. After the one-month minimum the rent rolls monthly, and you give one month's notice when the work is finished.

Can a sports club rent a unit?

Yes, with our prior approval for the use. One named person signs up with valid photo ID and a Direct Debit mandate, and the club can share the cost however it likes.

Can I store a mobility scooter or e-bike?

Bikes and household items are fine. For anything with a large battery, ask us on WhatsApp first so we can talk it through.

Can I store petrol mowers or fuel cans from the garden?

Bulk fuel is not allowed unless agreed in writing. Ask us before bringing anything that contains fuel.
