Useful information

Common
questions

Can I use the address on my Google Business Profile?

Yes. Tenants can use The Old Rock Factory, Keswick Road, Blackpool FY1 5PB as their business address on Google, so customers can find you without you putting your home address online.

How much notice do I give to leave?

One month. You rent month by month, with a minimum term of one month, so you are never locked into a long lease.

Can I move to a bigger or smaller unit?

Yes, at any time, subject to availability. As you grow you can also rent two or three adjoining units, and some have interconnecting doors so you can move between them without going outside.

Do I pay any utility deposits?

No. Lighting, electricity and Wi-Fi are already connected. You pay for the electricity you use at the supplier rate, with no markup. The only deposit is the security deposit of one month’s rent.

Can I rent more than one unit?

Yes, subject to availability. Some units have interconnecting doors, so one tenant can rent two or three adjoining units and move between them without going outside. Side-by-side units may also give you both roller-shutter and personnel-door access.

Can I run my business from a unit?

Small businesses, workshops and artist studios are welcome, subject to prior approval. Tenants can receive mail and clients on site. Tell us about your proposed activity so we can check whether the space and our buildings insurance are suitable. Car storage is not permitted.

Do the units have windows?

All ground-floor areas are windowless. The 300 sq ft two-storey unit includes an upstairs office or storage area with a window.

What do I pay before moving in?

You’ll need one month’s rent in advance and a security deposit equal to one month’s rent. You’ll also need valid government-issued photographic identification and a signed Direct Debit mandate for ongoing rent. Electricity is charged separately according to usage.
